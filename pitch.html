The inversion

Today Hospital → searches for → patient Adepe Patient → searches for → study

The direction of the search decides who has to disclose. When the sponsor is looking, every candidate opens their chart to be considered. When the patient is looking, they only publish one bit: this person satisfies HORIZON‑1. Same match, none of the exposure, and the funnel starts with people who already know they qualify.

Four steps, one proof.

01

Hospital signs

A registered provider Schnorr-signs the medical history — age and five oncology criteria — and hands it to the patient. On-chain, only the provider's public key is registered.

02

Patient holds

The signed record lives in the patient's wallet as a witness. It is never uploaded, never escrowed, never sent to a sponsor.

03

Circuit checks

Verify(trialID) validates the signature, confirms the signer is a registered provider, and compares the record against the trial's criteria — all inside the proof.

04

Pseudonym enrolls

A trial-specific key, hashed from the patient's secret and the trial ID, joins that trial's eligible set. The sponsor sees a qualified enrollee and can invite them.

The enrollment key mixes in the trial ID on purpose: a patient eligible for four studies appears as four unrelated keys, so nobody can join the public sets to rebuild one person's trial history. Re-proving is idempotent.
